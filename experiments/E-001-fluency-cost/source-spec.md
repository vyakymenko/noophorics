# Meridian Review — Submission Handling Rules (2026 round)

*Source material for [E-001](PREREGISTRATION.md). Fictional. Given in full to
the sender and to the CEILING receiver; never to the treatment receivers.*

> **Domain replaced 2026-07-28.** The original domain (Kestrel Fellowship
> eligibility) was unusable: it tripped a `cyber`-category safety classifier
> and the sender could not compose a brief at all. The logical structure below
> is identical, rule for rule; only the semantics changed. See
> [AMENDMENT-001.md](AMENDMENT-001.md).

The submission deadline is **1 March 2026**. Every rule below is evaluated as
of that date.

---

**R1 — Length.** A manuscript must be at least 22 pages. Exactly 22 pages
satisfies this rule.

**R2 — Format.** A manuscript must either use the official template, or carry
at least 3 years of documented revision history.

**R3 — Venue overlap.** A manuscript presented at a Tier-1 venue is not
handled, unless the presentation was *more than* 18 months before the
deadline. Exactly 18 months does not qualify.

**R4 — Prior rounds.** A manuscript accepted in a prior Meridian round is not
handled if *fewer than* 5 years have elapsed since that round. Exactly 5 years
elapsed is fine.

**R5 — Paired submissions.** A paired submission is permitted for exactly 2
manuscripts; 3 or more is not a permitted form. Both manuscripts must
independently satisfy R1 and R2. At least one of the two must satisfy R3; it
is not required that both do.

**R6 — Track D.** A manuscript submitted to Track D is exempt from R2. In
place of R2 it must carry exactly 2 endorsements; 1 endorsement is
insufficient. R6 exempts the manuscript from R2 only, and from no other rule.

**R7 — Retraction.** A manuscript with an open retraction notice is not
handled, regardless of every other rule in this document, including R9.

**R8 — Figures.** The figure count must be between 8 and 45 inclusive. A
paired submission may carry up to 70 figures, with the same minimum of 8.

**R9 — Figure adjustment.** A submission that fails R8 *only* by carrying
fewer than the minimum is padded up to the minimum rather than returned; its
verdict is PADDED. A submission carrying more than the applicable maximum is
returned outright.

**R10 — Withdrawals.** A withdrawal in the prior round does not count as an
acceptance under R4. However, a withdrawal resets the R3 venue clock: the
presentation is treated as having occurred on the withdrawal date rather than
on its actual date.

---

## Verdicts

Every submission resolves to exactly one of:

- **HANDLED** — satisfies all applicable rules.
- **RETURNED** — fails at least one applicable rule.
- **PADDED** — used *only* for the R9 case: the submission would be handled
  but carries fewer figures than the minimum, and is padded up rather than
  returned. A submission that would be PADDED but also fails another rule is
  RETURNED.
