# The Kestrel Fellowship — Eligibility Rules (2026 cycle)

*Source material for [E-001](PREREGISTRATION.md). Fictional. Given in full to
the sender and to the CEILING receiver; never to the treatment receivers.*

The application deadline is **1 March 2026**. Every rule below is evaluated as
of that date.

---

**R1 — Age.** An applicant must be at least 22 years old on the deadline.
Turning 22 on the deadline itself satisfies this rule.

**R2 — Qualification.** An applicant must either hold a completed degree, or
have at least 3 years of documented practice in the field.

**R3 — Tier-1 affiliation.** An applicant affiliated with a Tier-1 institution
is ineligible, unless the affiliation ended *more than* 18 months before the
deadline. Exactly 18 months does not qualify.

**R4 — Prior awards.** A prior Kestrel recipient is ineligible if *fewer than*
5 years have elapsed since their award year. Exactly 5 years elapsed is
eligible.

**R5 — Joint applications.** A joint application is permitted for exactly 2
applicants; 3 or more is not a permitted form. Both applicants must
independently satisfy R1 and R2. At least one of the two must satisfy R3; it
is not required that both do.

**R6 — Region D.** An applicant whose primary residence is in Region D is
exempt from R2. In place of R2 they must supply exactly 2 letters of support
from prior Kestrel recipients; 1 letter is insufficient. R6 exempts the
applicant from R2 only, and from no other rule.

**R7 — Ethics.** An applicant with an open ethics finding is ineligible,
regardless of every other rule in this document, including R9.

**R8 — Budget.** The requested budget must be between 8,000 and 45,000 units
inclusive. A joint application may request up to 70,000 units, with the same
8,000 minimum.

**R9 — Budget adjustment.** An application that fails R8 *only* by requesting
less than the minimum is auto-adjusted up to the minimum rather than rejected;
its verdict is ADJUSTED. An application requesting more than the applicable
maximum is rejected outright.

**R10 — Withdrawals.** An application withdrawn in the previous cycle does not
count as a prior award under R4. However, a withdrawal resets the R3
affiliation clock: the affiliation is treated as having ended on the withdrawal
date rather than on its actual end date.

---

## Verdicts

Every case resolves to exactly one of:

- **ELIGIBLE** — satisfies all applicable rules.
- **INELIGIBLE** — fails at least one applicable rule.
- **ADJUSTED** — used *only* for the R9 case: the application would be eligible
  but requested below the minimum, and is adjusted up rather than rejected. An
  application that would be ADJUSTED but also fails another rule is INELIGIBLE.
